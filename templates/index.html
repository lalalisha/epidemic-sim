MATHEMATICAL MODELS
SIR Model — Ordinary Differential Equations
dS/dt = −β·S·I/N − ν·S
dI/dt = +β·S·I/N − γ·I − μ·I
dR/dt = +γ·I + ν·S
dD/dt = +μ·I

R₀ = β_eff / γ = β·(1−δ) / γ
Herd Immunity Threshold = 1 − 1/R₀
SEIR Model — Extended with Exposed Compartment
dS/dt = −β·S·I/N − ν·S
dE/dt = +β·S·I/N − σ·E
dI/dt = +σ·E − γ·I − μ·I
dR/dt = +γ·I + ν·S
dD/dt = +μ·I

Mean incubation period = 1/σ days
Mean infectious period = 1/γ days
Parameter Reference
N — Total Population
β — Transmission rate (contacts × prob)
γ — Recovery rate (1/γ = infectious days)
σ — Incubation rate [SEIR only]
ν — Vaccination rate per day
μ — Disease-induced mortality rate
δ — Social distancing factor [0,1]
R₀ Interpretation
R₀ > 1 — Epidemic grows (outbreak)
R₀ = 1 — Endemic equilibrium
R₀ < 1 — Epidemic dies out

COVID-19: R₀ ≈ 2.5–3.5
Influenza: R₀ ≈ 1.2–1.4
Measles: R₀ ≈ 12–18
